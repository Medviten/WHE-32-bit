# mod-worgoblin-high-elf
## F-086_travel_form

### Features
- Replaces the WotLK travel form (the cheetah) with later looks
    - Mists of Pandaria travel forms, Alliance and Horde, for every race that can be a druid (ARAC included)
    - Battle for Azeroth race forms for the Zandalari Troll and Kul Tiran (by hair color)

### Known Issues
- Looks only: the form still behaves as the WotLK travel form.

### Considerations
- Without this modpak, every race uses the stock cheetah.
- Run it after the race modpaks and F-011/F-084 (as in the manifest), so their druid races are in charbaseinfo.
- Needs the client's travel form models and textures (patch-A).
