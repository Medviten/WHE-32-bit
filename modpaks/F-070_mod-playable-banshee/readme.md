# mod-worgoblin-high-elf
## F-070_mod-playable-banshee

### Features
- Playable Banshees (Horde), one body for both genders
    - CoA Custom 1.9 model and textures (Ilusixn's CoA race files, CoA race 90), prepared by `tools/models/coa_race_prep.py`
    - Customization: the 6/6 skin colours of CoA's appearance catalog (`EsteriaBanshee.bin`); its other options (EsteriaAppearance.dll) are fixed at their first choice
    - Home: Undead (classes, start position, start outfits, action bars, stats, totems, druid forms, race-restricted
      quests and items)
    - Kin: Undead (voice, emotes and names, copied at build time)
    - Helmet prefix 'Be' (CoA's)
    - Model size and display scale from CoA's CreatureModelData/CreatureDisplayInfo rows
- Generated by `tools/races/coa_race_modpaks.py` (tentative, for testing)

### Requirements
- Client files from `tools/models/coa_race_prep.py` (in WHE-common's `patch-A.MPQ`)
- Custom client with `creatureSkinRaces` containing the race ID: the skin texture is composited whole and armour textures are not drawn on it
- Interface: race button and the BANSHEE entries in `CharacterCreate.lua/.xml`, `GlueParent.lua` and `GlueStrings.lua`

### Known Issues
- Tentative: not tested in game yet
- No racial mount
- Placeholder race icon
- Barber shop styles have generic names
- Character creation camera is not tuned for the model's size
- CoA's extra appearance options (EsteriaAppearance.dll) are not available
- One body (CoA: female only): both genders use it, with the Kin race's voices

### CoA Custom 1.9 racials
- Active: learned with the racial skill line
- From CoA race 120 (Banshee), renumbered; blocks at the end of `[BASE,F-070]_spell.sql`, `_skilllineability.sql` and `_spellicon.sql`
    - Deathless Shroud: The dead feel no cold. Reduces Frost and Shadow damage taken by 3%.
    - Wailing Gale: A keening wind carries you onward. Increases your movement speed by 3%.
    - Spectral Sway: Half in the grave, half in the air. Increases your chance to dodge by 3%.
    - Banshee Wail: A wail that freezes the living: stuns all enemies within 8 yards for 2 sec.
    - Spectral Glide: Slip between worlds: removes snares and roots and hardens your spirit, reducing damage taken for 8 sec.
