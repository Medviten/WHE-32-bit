# mod-worgoblin-high-elf
## F-085_tree_of_life

### Features
- Replaces the WotLK Tree of Life (the treant, known as Treant Form in retail) with Cataclysm's lumbering oak
    - Race colors: Night Elf purple, Tauren and Highmountain Tauren green, Troll orange, Worgen, Gilnean and
      Lightforged Draenei red; Zandalari Troll and Kul Tiran get their own Battle for Azeroth oaks
    - Every other race that can be a druid gets the purple oak, unless another modpak gave it a tree form of its own
      (F-011_mod-druid-forms' Undead ents and Gnome lashers stay)

### Known Issues
- None

### Considerations
- Without this modpak, every race uses the stock treant.
- Run it after the race modpaks and F-011/F-084 (as in the manifest), so their druid races are in charbaseinfo.
- Needs the client's tree form models and textures (patch-A).
