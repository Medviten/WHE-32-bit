# mod-worgoblin-high-elf
## F-065_mod-playable-sylvari

### Features
- Playable Sylvari (Alliance)
    - CoA Custom 1.9 model and textures (Ilusixn's CoA race files, CoA race 110), prepared by `tools/models/coa_race_prep.py`
    - Customization: the 8/8 skin colours of CoA's appearance catalog (`EsteriaVorkai.bin`); its other options (EsteriaAppearance.dll) are fixed at their first choice
    - Home: NightElf (classes, start position, start outfits, action bars, stats, totems, druid forms, race-restricted
      quests and items)
    - Kin: NightElf (voice, emotes and names, copied at build time)
    - Helmet prefix 'Be' (CoA's)
    - Model size and display scale from CoA's CreatureModelData/CreatureDisplayInfo rows
- Generated by `tools/races/coa_race_modpaks.py` (tentative, for testing)

### Requirements
- Client files from `tools/models/coa_race_prep.py` (in WHE-common's `patch-A.MPQ`)
- Custom client with `creatureSkinRaces` containing the race ID: the skin texture is composited whole and armour textures are not drawn on it
- Interface: race button and the SYLVARI entries in `CharacterCreate.lua/.xml`, `GlueParent.lua` and `GlueStrings.lua`

### Known Issues
- Tentative: not tested in game yet
- No racial mount
- Placeholder race icon
- Barber shop styles have generic names
- Character creation camera is not tuned for the model's size
- CoA's extra appearance options (EsteriaAppearance.dll) are not available

### CoA Custom 1.9 racials
- Active: learned with the racial skill line
- From CoA race 114 (Sylvari), renumbered; blocks at the end of `[BASE,F-065]_spell.sql`, `_skilllineability.sql` and `_spellicon.sql`
    - Thornhide: Bark-hard fur turns aside the wild. Reduces Nature damage taken by 3%.
    - Fleet of Hoof: Cloven hooves find purchase on any trail. Increases your movement speed by 3%.
    - Fey Grace: The Sylvari move like deer through the brush. Increases your chance to dodge by 3%.
    - Moonlit Sight: Eyes that never lose the quarry in the dark. Increases your chance to critically strike with melee and ranged attacks by 2%.
    - Verdant Renewal: Wild growth knits your wounds, healing you over 15 sec. The amount grows with your level.
