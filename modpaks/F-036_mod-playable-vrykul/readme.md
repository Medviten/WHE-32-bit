# mod-worgoblin-high-elf
## F-036_mod-vrykul

### Features
- Playable Vrykul (Alliance)
    - All ten classes
    - Customization, barber shop styles and helmet geosets from Kalibros' Vrykul client files

### Known Issues
- No racial mount
- No druid forms
- No sound yet
- Racial faction name and description are placeholders

### CoA Custom 1.9 racials
- Active: learned with the racial skill line
- From CoA race 18 (Vrykul), renumbered; blocks at the end of `[BASE,F-036]_spell.sql`, `_skilllineability.sql` and `_spellicon.sql`
    - Fury of the Val'kyr: Increases your attack power by $s1 and your spell power by $s2. Lasts $d. Cannot be used while offensive effects from trinkets are active.
    - Titan Blood: Base Health increased by $s1%.
    - Born in the Cold: Reduces the damage taken by Frost spells by $s1%.
    - Axe of the Fjord: Expertise with with Fist Weapons, Axes and Two-Handed Axes increased by $s1.
