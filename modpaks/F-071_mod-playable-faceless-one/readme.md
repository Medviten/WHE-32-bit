# mod-worgoblin-high-elf
## F-071_mod-playable-faceless-one

### Features
- Playable Faceless Ones (Horde), male only
    - CoA Custom 1.9 model and textures (Ilusixn's CoA race files, CoA race 87), prepared by `tools/models/coa_race_prep.py`
    - Customization: the 9 skin colours of CoA's appearance catalog (`EsteriaFaceless.bin`); its other options (EsteriaAppearance.dll) are fixed at their first choice
    - Home: Orc (classes, start position, start outfits, action bars, stats, totems, druid forms, race-restricted
      quests and items)
    - Kin: Undead (voice, emotes and names, copied at build time)
    - Helmet prefix 'Gn' (CoA's)
    - Model size and display scale from CoA's CreatureModelData/CreatureDisplayInfo rows
- Generated by `tools/races/coa_race_modpaks.py` (tentative, for testing)

### Requirements
- Client files from `tools/models/coa_race_prep.py` (in WHE-common's `patch-A.MPQ`)
- Custom client with `creatureSkinRaces` containing the race ID: the skin texture is composited whole and armour textures are not drawn on it
- Interface: race button and the FACELESSONE entries in `CharacterCreate.lua/.xml`, `GlueParent.lua` and `GlueStrings.lua`

### Known Issues
- Tentative: not tested in game yet
- No racial mount
- Placeholder race icon
- Barber shop styles have generic names
- Character creation camera is not tuned for the model's size
- CoA's extra appearance options (EsteriaAppearance.dll) are not available
- Male only: female characters use the male model and customization (F-026's `patches/core/male-only-races.patch` creates them male)

### CoA Custom 1.9 racials
- Active: learned with the racial skill line
- From CoA race 122 (Faceless One), renumbered; blocks at the end of `[BASE,F-071]_spell.sql`, `_skilllineability.sql` and `_spellicon.sql`
    - Faceless Hide: A hide that drinks the light. Reduces Shadow and Nature damage taken by 3%.
    - Creeping Dread: You glide where others walk. Increases your movement speed by 3%.
    - Formless: No face, no weak point. Increases your chance to dodge by 3%.
    - Shapeless Squirm: Without a face there is nothing to hold: escape all immobilizing and movement-slowing effects.
    - Mind Rot: Silences all enemies within 8 yards for 2 sec and restores energy or mana.
